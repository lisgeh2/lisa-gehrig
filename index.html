    why make a song with me?

    So many people carry a story or a feeling that wants to become a song. A melody hummed on the way home, a few lines in a notes app, a chorus that won't leave you alone. Most of them never get a form.

    Helping people give it one has been my dream for years. Sitting down together and finding the song that's already in there: the chords, the melody, the words that are actually true.

    how it works

    you bring
    a feeling, a story, a voice memo, half a chorus. Anything.
    we explore
    playfully, without judgement. We try things, keep what feels true, let go of the rest.
    we shape it
    layer by layer, until a vague idea becomes something specific you can hold, play and send to people.

    why with me

    by ear
    I hear harmony fast, so we spend our time on your song, not on theory.
    start to finish
    writing, arranging, recording, mixing, mastering: all in one place.
    psychology
    I'm doing my master's at UZH. I care about making a space where honest feelings are welcome.
    free
    I don't charge for this. I do it because I love it.

    AI can generate a song in seconds now. But so much of the joy is in the craft: in the process, in hearing music turn into an experience, layer by layer. I'd love to share that with you.

    more about me

    I'm Lisa. I play piano, a lot. Also some guitar and ukulele, a bit of bass and drums, and I sing.

    My favourite game: put on a random song, take it apart by ear as fast as I can (chords, inversions, melodies, countermelodies), play it back, and then jam with it.
